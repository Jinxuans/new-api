# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and teams use the hosted service to obtain API access, compare model availability and pricing, recharge accounts, monitor usage, and manage keys. Operators and administrators use the same product to manage billing, rewards, risk, support, and service reliability.

## Product Purpose

Provide a trustworthy commercial AI API service with clear account, pricing, recharge, model, usage, reward, and support workflows. Success means customers can understand what they own, what changed, and what action to take without learning internal billing or provider concepts.

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, and operationally calm. Copy is direct and specific, with enough context for financial and administrative decisions but no promotional filler inside task-focused surfaces.

## Anti-references

- A generic self-hosting template that makes the official service feel unfinished.
- A noisy crypto-style rewards dashboard with speculative language or decorative finance visuals.
- Dense administration screens that expose database terminology or require operators to infer hidden workflow order.
- Ambiguous balance labels, status-only history, and actions whose financial effect is not stated.

## Design Principles

- Show the source, destination, status, and consequence of every financial action.
- Keep common customer actions obvious while preserving drill-down detail for support and audit work.
- Use one consistent vocabulary across customer and administrator surfaces.
- Prefer explicit recovery and error states over optimistic or silent completion.
- Preserve operational trust through accessible, responsive, and reversible interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus order, accessible names, and form errors. Respect reduced-motion preferences, never rely on color alone for financial status, and keep long translated labels usable across all supported locales.
